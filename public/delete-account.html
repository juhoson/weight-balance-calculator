Delete your account

Flight Planning Tool, by Songer Consulting AB: the website www.flightplanningtool.com and the iOS and Android apps. Radera ditt konto och dess data.

You can delete your Flight Planning Tool account and the data kept with it yourself, at any time, from a browser or from the app. It takes effect at once.

Delete my account

Opens Flight Planning Tool. Sign in with the account you want to delete (email and password, Google, or Apple), then confirm.

Steps

  1. Open www.flightplanningtool.com/?delete-account, or in the app open your account menu and choose Profile.
  2. Sign in, if you aren't already.
  3. Under Delete account, type DELETE and choose Delete for good.

What is deleted

  • Your account: email address, name, password hash, and the link to Google or Apple sign-in.
  • Your own aircraft and their weighing data, your saved routes and saved flights (GPS tracks).
  • Your club memberships, and every signed-in session on every device.

What is kept

  • Aircraft you added to a club stay with the club for its members, without your name.
  • Nothing else is kept. Data on your own device (the planned route, settings) goes when you clear the browser's site data or uninstall the app.

Can't sign in?

Reset your password from the sign-in screen first. If that doesn't work, ask us through songerconsulting.se to delete the account, from the email address it uses.
