Privacy policy

Flight Planning Tool: the website www.flightplanningtool.com and the iOS and Android apps. Last updated 26 November 2026.

Flight Planning Tool is provided by Songer Consulting AB (Sweden), which is responsible for the personal data described here. This page says what we collect, why, who else handles it, and how you remove it.

Without an account

You can calculate weight and balance and plan routes without an account. What you enter is kept on your own device (in the browser's or the app's storage) and is not sent to us, except the airports and route you look up, which our server needs to fetch weather, airspace and airport information. Those lookups are not linked to you.

Your account

  • Email address and name: to sign you in, and to send password reset and club invitation emails.
  • Password: stored only as a one-way hash.
  • Google or Apple sign-in: if you use it, we receive your email address and name from Google or Apple, a stable account identifier, and with Google your profile picture link. With Sign in with Apple you may hide your email, and we then receive an Apple relay address.
  • What you save: your aircraft and their weighing data, saved routes, club memberships, and the navigation mode's terms you accepted.

Location

The navigation mode, and "Frequencies where I am", use your device's location, and only while they are open on screen. Positions are used on your device to show where you are. The flight's track stays on your device unless you choose to save the flight to your account. We do not track your location in the background.

Documents you upload

When you add an aircraft from a weighing report, the document is sent to our AI provider (Anthropic) to read the values, which you then check. It is not used to train AI models.

Usage statistics (website only)

The website counts page views and a few actions (such as opening the navigation mode) with Umami, a privacy-friendly analytics service that uses no cookies and stores no IP addresses. The apps do not send usage statistics.

Who else handles the data

  • Railway: hosts our servers and database.
  • Resend: delivers our emails.
  • Google and Apple: sign-in, if you choose it.
  • Anthropic: reads uploaded weighing reports.
  • Weather, airspace and map data providers: receive the areas and routes looked up, not who you are.

We do not sell personal data, show ads, or track you across other apps and websites.

How long, and deleting it

Your account data is kept until you delete your account. You can do that yourself at any time: sign in, open Profile, and choose Delete account, or go straight there with this link. It removes your account, your own aircraft, saved routes and flights, and signs you out everywhere, at once and for good. Data on your device goes when you clear the browser's site data or uninstall the app.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you may ask for a copy of your data, have it corrected or erased, object to its use, and complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY, imy.se). Contact us through songerconsulting.se.
